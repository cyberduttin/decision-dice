Privacy Policy — Decision Dice

Effective date: August 31, 2026

This privacy policy covers Decision Dice (package id: com.infiniteimprobability.decisiondice), an Android app published by INFINITE IMPROBABILITY LLC.

Decision Dice is a single-screen decision-maker: you tap a button, it shows a random verdict (YES, NO, or ASK AGAIN) and a playful “improbability coefficient.” That is the whole app — and that simplicity extends to your privacy.

The short version

Decision Dice collects no data. None. Zero.

Data we collect

None. Decision Dice does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data, usage data, device data, or any other information about you or your device.

Data we share

None. Since the app collects nothing, there is nothing to share — with anyone, ever.

How the app works

  • Fully offline. Decision Dice does not request internet access and cannot communicate over the internet.
  • No permissions. The app requests no Android permissions at all.
  • No accounts. There is no sign-up, login, or account of any kind.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no advertising, no cookies, no identifiers.
  • No third-party code that collects data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

All randomness is generated on your device and never leaves it.

Children

Decision Dice is intended for adults aged 18 and over. The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone — children included — because it collects no data from any user whatsoever.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Decision Dice handles data (for example, in a future update), we will update this policy and revise the effective date above before that change takes effect. The current version of this policy will always be available at this public URL.
